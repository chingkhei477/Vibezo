Quick answers

Good to know before you shop.

The full rules live in our Cashback Policy.

Where does Vibezo cashback come from?

Retailers pay Vibezo a commission when you shop through our link. We share part of that commission with you as cashback, at the rate shown on each offer.

When does my cashback get approved?

After the retailer confirms your order — usually once its return or cancellation window has closed. Each offer shows that retailer's usual approval time.

Why might cashback be declined?

Most often because the order was cancelled, returned or refunded, a coupon wasn't allowed, or the order didn't meet the offer's conditions. Our Cashback Policy lists every reason.

How do I withdraw?

When your approved cashback reaches ₹100, request a payout to a UPI ID in your own name. UPI is the only payout method.

My cashback didn't track. What can I do?

Wait until the offer's tracking time has passed, then send us your order ID, order date, amount and the retailer's confirmation email. We'll take it up with the retailer.

Got a favourite store?

Tell us where you'd love to earn cashback. Your suggestions help us decide which retailers to bring to Vibezo next.

Tracking tips

Six habits that help your cashback track.

Tracking depends on the retailer recognising that you came from Vibezo. These give it the best chance.

  • Start from Vibezo

    Tap “Shop via Vibezo” right before you buy, even if you've visited the store already.

  • Finish in one go

    Pay in the same browser session you opened from Vibezo.

  • Pause ad blockers

    Some blockers and strict privacy modes stop the retailer recording your visit.

  • Use allowed coupons only

    Codes from other websites can cancel cashback. Check the offer's conditions.

  • Fill your cart after tapping

    Items already in your cart before the visit may not track.

  • Keep your order email

    You'll need the order ID if you ever report missing cashback.

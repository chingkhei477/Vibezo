About Vibezo

Cashback that feels good.

Vibezo is a cashback and rewards platform for shoppers in India, based in Manipur.

Getting money back should feel like a little treat — not homework. So Vibezo tells you the rate, shows you every condition, and lets you see exactly where your cashback is.

What we stand for

Four things we always do.

Keep it clear

Every offer shows its rate, conditions, tracking time and approval time on the card itself.

List only verified offers

An offer goes live only when tracking works and its terms are confirmed with the retailer.

Respect your data

We collect only what's needed to track and pay cashback and to reply to you — and never sell it.

Answer like humans

Questions, missing cashback and payouts go to a real inbox, read by real people.

How Vibezo earns

Your cashback comes from our commission.

Retailers pay Vibezo a commission when a purchase made through our link is confirmed. We pass part of that commission on to you as cashback. That's the whole model.

Because cashback comes from the retailer's commission, it's paid only after the retailer approves your order.
